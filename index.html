Church Life and Ministries

The Church

A Christ-centered community rooted in worship, fellowship, service and spiritual growth.

Learn More

Edavaka Sangham

A fellowship that brings parish members together for worship, service, spiritual growth and active participation in the life of the church.

Sunday School

A nurturing space where children learn Scripture, faith values, worship practices and Christian living through engaging lessons and activities.

Youth League

A vibrant fellowship for young people to grow in faith, leadership, service and community through worship, learning and activities.

Choir

A dedicated group that leads the congregation in worship through music, hymns and meaningful participation in church services.

Sevika Sangham

A fellowship focused on prayer, service, spiritual growth and active support for church ministries and community care.

Senior Citizen Fellowship

A warm fellowship for senior members to gather in prayer, encouragement, companionship and continued participation in church life.

Parish Appointment: 2024 - Present

Rev. John Mathew was ordained to the priesthood in the Malankara Mar Thoma Syrian Church and has faithfully served parish congregations in Kerala and across South India.

His ministry emphasizes pastoral visitation, youth mentoring, spiritual counseling, and fostering active lay leadership within the church. He lives in the parish parsonage with his family.
