About Our Vicar

Rev. K.A. Varghese serves Jerusalem Mar Thoma Church Hebbal with a heart for faith, fellowship, and pastoral care. His ministry is centred on nurturing spiritual growth, encouraging meaningful relationships, and guiding the parish community in worship and service.

He seeks to build a Christ-centred parish family where every member feels welcomed, valued, and encouraged to participate in the life and mission of the church.

Through prayer, teaching, and pastoral leadership, Rev. K.A. Varghese continues to support the parish in growing together as a community rooted in faith, love, and service.

Message from Our Vicar

“

Dear Parish Family,

It is a blessing and privilege to serve Jerusalem Mar Thoma Church Hebbal.

My prayer is that our parish continues to grow in faith, fellowship, worship, and service to our Lord.

May we walk together in Christian love, support one another, and remain rooted in God’s Word.

With prayers and blessings,

Rev. K.A. Varghese
