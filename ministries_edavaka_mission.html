Proclaiming the Gospel and extending Christ’s love through prayer, witness, and parish outreach.

About the Edavaka Mission

The Edavaka Mission is the parish branch of the Mar Thoma Voluntary Evangelistic Association. Its core mission is to encourage every parishioner to be an active witness for Jesus Christ in their daily lives, family, workplace, and local community.

The ministry organizes weekly cottage prayer meetings, intercessory prayer gatherings, Bible studies, and home visitation programs across all prayer groups in the Hebbal parish.

Through charitable outreach, medical assistance, gospel literature distribution, and annual parish conventions, the Edavaka Mission actively serves both spiritual and community needs.

We invite all members of the parish to join hands in spreading the Gospel message of hope, peace, and salvation.
