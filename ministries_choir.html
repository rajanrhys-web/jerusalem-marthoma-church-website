About the Choir

The Choir Ministry exists to glorify God through uplifting music and worship. We lead the congregation in praise, enhance worship services and foster a spirit of unity and devotion through music.

The choir actively participates in church services, retreats, special programs, choir festivals and outreach initiatives.

The ministry provides opportunities for fellowship, discipleship and spiritual growth through service.

The choir welcomes members who desire to use their gifts for the glory of God and the strengthening of the church community.
