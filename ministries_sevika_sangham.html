Sevika Sangham

Empowering women in faith, prayer, charitable service, and parish ministry.

About the Sevika Sangham

The Mar Thoma Suvisesha Sevika Sangham is the women’s ministry of the Mar Thoma Church. It plays a vital role in the spiritual enrichment, fellowship, and mission of Jerusalem Mar Thoma Church Hebbal.

The ministry engages women in intercessory prayer, Bible study, family counseling, house visits, and providing support for bereaved or sick families in our parish.

Sevika Sangham members actively organize charitable activities, educational scholarships for needy students, food sales for mission funds, and support for diocesan mission projects.

We warmly invite all women of the parish to take part in these monthly meetings and spiritual retreats.
