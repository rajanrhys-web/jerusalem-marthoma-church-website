OUR PARISH

A vibrant worshipping community since 1990

Jerusalem Mar Thoma Church, Hebbal, Bengaluru is a parish of the Mar Thoma Syrian Church of Malabar under the Chennai–Bangalore Diocese. Since its beginning in 1990, the parish has prayerfully grown into a vibrant worshipping community serving families across North Bengaluru.

We celebrate Holy Communion every Sunday at 8:00 AM (Malayalam) and 10:30 AM (English).

Parish History

The humble beginnings of our parish and the journey of faith that shaped it.

1990

Formation of the Parish

A joint meeting on 11 December 1990 of members from three Bangalore Mar Thoma parishes led the Hebbal and J.C. Nagar prayer groups to initiate a new parish. With the blessing of Rt. Rev. Dr. Geevarghese Mar Theodosius, the Bangalore North Congregation was constituted; worship began at R.T. Nagar under Rev. M. S. Skariah.

1991

Independent Parish Status

A 110×65 ft plot was acquired on Kempapura Road, Hebbal, through Late Rev. K. Thomas, and a 22×40 ft chapel was built. The congregation was elevated to an independent parish on 1 August 1991, with Late Rev. K. Thomas as first Vicar.

1992

Foundation Stone Laid

The foundation stone for the present church was laid on 8 May 1992 by Rt. Rev. Dr. Geevarghese Mar Theodosius Episcopa.

1993

Basement Hall Dedicated

The basement hall was dedicated on 18 March 1993, and worship continued there as the main church rose above.

1994

Main Church Dedicated

The main church was dedicated on 21 May 1994 by Most Rev. Dr. Alexander Mar Thoma Metropolitan, assisted by Rt. Rev. Dr. Philipose Mar Chrysostom, Rt. Rev. Dr. Geevarghese Mar Theodosius and Rt. Rev. Thomas Mar Timotheos, with faithful from all Bangalore parishes.
